Illustrative application示例应用

Three delirium prediction models, read through the taxonomy用分类法解读三个谵妄预测模型

To examine whether the dimensions can be recognized in concrete prediction model reports, we applied the taxonomy informally to three delirium prediction studies listed in the supplementary material of a recent scoping review of machine learning for delirium prediction using electronic health records. The exercise was intended to demonstrate interpretability of the taxonomy, not to estimate the frequency of communication problems in delirium prediction.为检验这些维度能否在具体的预测模型报告中被识别出来，我们把分类法非正式地应用于三项谵妄预测研究。这三项研究来自近期一篇关于用电子健康记录和机器学习预测谵妄的范围综述的补充材料。这项练习旨在展示分类法的可解释性，而不是估计谵妄预测中沟通问题的发生频率。

A clinician receives an individualized estimate without information about the low base rate, precision-recall performance, or how those characteristics should shape interpretation. Making a model accessible is not equivalent to making the reliability of its output interpretable.临床医生拿到一个个体化的估计，却不知道基础发生率很低、精确率-召回率表现如何，也不知道这些特点应当如何影响解读。让模型可以使用，并不等于让它输出的可靠性可以被理解。

Age and Ageing · 2024

Delirium prediction component for a geriatric co-management system (SURGE-Ahead), with clinician-facing communication requirements as explicit design goals.老年共同管理系统（SURGE-Ahead）中的谵妄预测组件，明确把面向临床医生的沟通要求作为设计目标。

On the clinician-facing display面向临床医生的显示

  • A calibrated probability estimate, accompanied by the features contributing most to the prediction一个经过校准的概率估计，并附上对预测贡献最大的特征
  • Expressed as a probability rather than a categorical directive, preserving space for clinical interpretation以概率而非分类指令的形式表达，为临床解读留出空间

Reported in the manuscript论文中报告的

  • Internal-external cross-validation across centers跨中心的内部-外部交叉验证
  • Potential algorithmic bias assessed by sex and native language with the Aequitas framework; ethnicity data noted as unavailable用 Aequitas 框架按性别和母语评估了潜在的算法偏倚；并说明缺少种族数据
  • Published linear decision function, coefficients, normalization values, and the mean/median values used for missing inputs公开了线性决策函数、系数、标准化参数，以及缺失输入所用的均值或中位数

The study does not address every dimension of the taxonomy, but it illustrates that several communication dimensions can be addressed within the development and reporting of a clinical prediction model.这项研究并没有覆盖分类法的每个维度，但它说明，多个沟通维度可以在临床预测模型的开发和报告过程中得到处理。

doi:10.1093/ageing/afae101 ↗

These three cases are illustrative rather than systematic. They were not sampled for comparative evaluation, no formal coding instrument or inter-rater procedure was applied, and no claim is made about the frequency of any communication pattern in the broader delirium literature. They show that the dimensions can be applied to published prediction model reports and that they differentiate among communication approaches rather than labeling all studies identically.这三个案例是示例性的，而非系统性的。它们并非为比较评估而抽样，没有使用正式的编码工具或评分者间一致性程序，也不对更广泛的谵妄文献中任何沟通模式的频率作出判断。它们说明这些维度可以应用于已发表的预测模型报告，并且能区分不同的沟通方式，而不是给所有研究贴上相同的标签。
