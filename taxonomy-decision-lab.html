Decision lab决策实验室

Can a clearer display help clinicians read delirium risk?更清晰的显示，能帮助临床医生读懂谵妄风险吗？

A risk tool helps only if the people using it read its output correctly. The same output can be read well or poorly depending on how it is displayed, where it is used, how reliable it is, and how much attention it gets. This page tests that with clinicians first, then lets anyone explore why.风险工具只有在使用者正确读懂它的输出时才有用。同一个输出能否被读懂，取决于它如何显示、在哪里使用、有多可靠，以及得到多少注意力。这个页面先请临床医生做测试，然后让任何人都可以探索其中的原因。

1 · Build the pathway1 · 搭建路径

Drag the pieces of a deployment into place把部署的各个环节拖到对应位置

A prediction reaches a decision through a chain: the records the model was derived from, the setting it runs in, the patients it describes, the model itself, and the people who read its output. Uncertainty can be lost at each link, and fairness questions enter with the patient population.预测要经过一条链才能变成决策：模型所依据的记录、它运行的场景、它描述的患者、模型本身，以及阅读其输出的人。不确定性可能在每一环丢失，公平性问题则随着患者人群一起进入。

    3 · Model robustness3 · 模型稳健性

    Sample size, performance, and what a display leaves out样本量、性能，以及显示遗漏了什么

    A single performance number hides how sure we are about it and whether it still holds after deployment. Both demonstrations start from what Jauk et al. reported: an AUROC of 0.854 on the test data and 0.855 during seven months of prospective use, while calibration deteriorated.单一的性能数字掩盖了我们对它有多确定，以及它在部署后是否仍然成立。两个演示都以 Jauk 等报告的数据为起点：测试数据上 AUROC 为 0.854，七个月前瞻性使用期间为 0.855，而校准变差了。

Overstatement is modeled as a constant shift on the log-odds scale, which leaves the ranking of patients unchanged, and with it the AUROC and the percentile-based icons (red for the 95th to 100th percentile, yellow for the 85th to 94th).示例性的风险分布，并非该研究的数据。高估被建模为对数几率尺度上的固定偏移，这不会改变患者的排序，因此 AUROC 和基于百分位的图标（红色为第 95 至 100 百分位，黄色为第 85 至 94 百分位）都保持不变。

      Report performance with its uncertainty and the size of the evaluation behind it, and keep checking calibration after deployment. A display that encodes only rank cannot show drift, so reliability has to be communicated, not only measured.报告性能时要附上它的不确定性和评估的样本量，并在部署后持续检查校准。只编码排序的显示无法呈现漂移，所以可靠性不仅要被测量，还要被传达。

      4 · What if4 · 如果……会怎样

      What if the output does not get full attention?如果输出没有得到充分的注意会怎样？

      Alerts compete with interruptions, time pressure, and other alarms. Drag attention toward “rushed” and switch off what the display communicates. The less attention there is, the more the display has to carry on its own, and the more support the decision needs.警报要与打断、时间压力和其他报警争夺注意力。把注意力拖向“匆忙”，并关掉显示所传达的内容。注意力越少，显示就越需要自己把信息讲清楚，决策也就越需要支持。
