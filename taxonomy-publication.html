Abstract摘要

Healthcare decision-making increasingly relies on data-driven tools, including risk prediction models, clinical decision support systems, dashboards, and algorithmic recommendations. The clinical value of these tools depends not only on model performance but also on whether their outputs are communicated in ways that clinicians and patients can interpret, appropriately trust, and use. Reporting standards such as TRIPOD+AI specify what model developers should report to readers of the scientific literature, but they do not provide an equivalent framework for what a deployed model should communicate at the point of use. We developed a preliminary taxonomy of issues in healthcare data-driven communication through a structured synthesis of 48 sources identified by PubMed search and citation searching. The taxonomy comprises eight dimensions: risk information; uncertainty communication; presentation and format; patient and clinician interpretation; decision support; explainability and model communication; equity and subgroup reliability; and shared decision-making. Each dimension is linked to the clinical decision-making risk it poses and to a corresponding opportunity for intervention. Coverage in the coded corpus was uneven, with equity and subgroup reliability informed by a single study. An illustrative application to three published delirium prediction studies shows how the taxonomy can distinguish communication practices in concrete prediction model settings. These findings identify clinician- and patient-facing communication as an important and comparatively underspecified component of prediction model deployment.医疗决策越来越依赖数据驱动的工具，包括风险预测模型、临床决策支持系统、仪表盘和算法推荐。这些工具的临床价值不仅取决于模型性能，也取决于其输出是否以临床医生和患者能够理解、恰当信任并加以使用的方式传达。TRIPOD+AI 等报告规范规定了模型开发者应向科学文献读者报告的内容，却没有为已部署的模型在使用现场应当传达什么提供相应的框架。我们通过 PubMed 检索和引文追溯找到 48 篇文献，经结构化综合，构建了一个关于医疗数据驱动沟通问题的初步分类法。该分类法包括八个维度：风险信息；不确定性沟通；呈现与格式；患者与医生的解读；决策支持；可解释性与模型沟通；公平性与亚组可靠性；医患共同决策。每个维度都对应一种临床决策风险，以及一个相应的改进机会。编码文献在各维度上的覆盖并不均衡，公平性与亚组可靠性只有一项研究支持。将分类法示例性地应用于三项已发表的谵妄预测研究，展示了它能在具体的预测模型情境中区分不同的沟通做法。这些发现表明，面向临床医生和患者的沟通是预测模型部署中重要而相对缺乏规范的一环。

Methods方法

How the taxonomy was built分类法是如何构建的

We developed the taxonomy through a structured synthesis of the literature using a directed content analysis approach. An initial organizing structure, informed by prior work, was applied and iteratively revised as additional themes emerged. The objective was conceptual coverage of ways in which data-driven communication can break down in clinical decision-making, rather than exhaustive retrieval or estimation of the prevalence of individual issue types.我们采用定向内容分析的方法，通过对文献的结构化综合构建了这个分类法。先套用一个参考既往研究的初始框架，再随着新主题的出现不断修订。目标是从概念上覆盖数据驱动沟通在临床决策中可能出问题的方式，而不是穷尽检索，或估计各类问题的发生率。

PubMed search across three concept blocks跨三个概念组的 PubMed 检索

  • Communication of risk, probability, and uncertainty风险、概率与不确定性的沟通
  • AND
  • Healthcare and clinical settings, including the electronic health record医疗与临床情境，包括电子健康记录
  • AND
  • Artificial intelligence, prediction models, and clinical decision support人工智能、预测模型与临床决策支持

Terms were combined within blocks and intersected across them. Animal-only studies were removed with the filter animals[mh] NOT humans[mh].检索词在每个概念组内合并，再在各组之间取交集。仅涉及动物的研究用过滤条件 animals[mh] NOT humans[mh] 排除。

Purposive selection and citation searching目的性选择与引文追溯

  1. 128Records screened by title and abstract按标题和摘要筛选的记录

One coded paper also served as a conceptual anchor for the initial category structure.其中一篇编码文献同时作为初始类别结构的概念锚点。

Common extraction and iterative synthesis统一提取与迭代综合

Each coded source was characterized with a common extraction framework covering clinical and decision context, intended audience, the information communicated, communication format, uncertainty and reliability, human interpretation and decision implications, and an emerging taxonomy theme.每篇编码文献都用统一的提取框架描述，内容包括临床与决策情境、目标受众、所传达的信息、沟通形式、不确定性与可靠性、人的解读与决策含义，以及初步形成的分类主题。

Categories were merged, split, relabeled, and promoted to higher-level dimensions as the coded evidence suggested clearer boundaries. All screening, extraction, and initial thematic coding were performed by a single author (B.G.), and the category structure was reviewed and revised in discussion with the co-authors.随着编码证据显示出更清晰的边界，各类别被合并、拆分、重新命名，或提升为更高层级的维度。全部筛选、提取和初步主题编码由一位作者（B.G.）完成，类别结构则在与合著者的讨论中审阅和修订。

Limitations局限性

  • A directed, scoping synthesis rather than an exhaustive systematic review. The database search was limited to PubMed, which likely underrepresents studies indexed primarily in computing, visualization, and human factors venues.这是定向的范围综合，而非穷尽的系统综述。数据库检索仅限于 PubMed，可能遗漏主要收录在计算机、可视化和人因工程领域的研究。
  • Purposive selection. Only 10 of the 124 records judged topically relevant were advanced as seed papers. Uneven coverage across dimensions is not evidence that some problems are more common or important than others.目的性选择。在判定主题相关的 124 条记录中，只有 10 篇被选为种子文献。各维度覆盖不均，并不能说明某些问题比其他问题更常见或更重要。
  • A single coder. Individual records were not independently coded, and no inter-rater agreement statistics were computed.单人编码。记录没有经过独立的双人编码，也没有计算评分者间一致性。
  • A descriptive framework. The risk and intervention mappings in Table 4 have not been empirically validated.描述性框架。表 4 中风险与改进方向的对应关系尚未经过实证验证。
