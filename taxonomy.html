A Taxonomy of Issues in Healthcare Data-Driven Communication for Clinical Decision-Making面向临床决策的医疗数据驱动沟通问题分类法

Risk scores, prediction models, electronic health record summaries, dashboards, and algorithmic decision support systems now accompany clinicians at or near the point of care. A model can perform well statistically and still fail at the point of use if its output is difficult to interpret, invites inappropriate trust, or does not connect clearly to a decision.风险评分、预测模型、电子健康记录摘要、仪表盘和算法决策支持系统，如今已经出现在临床医生的床旁和诊疗现场。一个模型即使统计表现良好，如果它的输出难以理解、引发不恰当的信任，或与决策之间没有清晰的联系，到了实际使用时仍可能失效。

We developed a preliminary taxonomy of issues in healthcare data-driven communication through a structured synthesis of 48 sources. Each of its eight dimensions is linked to the clinical decision-making risk it poses and to a corresponding opportunity for intervention.我们通过对 48 篇文献的结构化综合，构建了一个关于医疗数据驱动沟通问题的初步分类法。它的八个维度各自对应一种临床决策风险，以及一个相应的改进机会。

The taxonomy分类法

Eight dimensions between a tool's output and a clinical decision从工具输出到临床决策之间的八个维度

The synthesis produced eight dimensions describing distinct but potentially overlapping ways in which data-driven communication can break down between a tool's output and a clinical decision. Each dimension is organized around its own analytic question and is mapped to a clinical decision-making risk and an opportunity for intervention.综合分析得出八个维度，描述数据驱动沟通在工具输出与临床决策之间可能出问题的不同方式；这些方式各不相同，但可能相互重叠。每个维度都围绕一个分析问题展开，并对应一种临床决策风险和一个改进机会。

Risk information风险信息

What estimate is being communicated?传达的是什么估计？

Focus关注点

The semantic content of the risk estimate itself, before considering how that estimate is displayed.风险估计本身的语义内容，先不考虑它如何显示。

  • Risk Estimate Ambiguity风险估计含糊

    • Confusion between absolute and relative risk混淆绝对风险与相对风险

  • Risk Stratification Problems风险分层问题

  • Clinical Meaning of Risk风险的临床含义

Clinical decision-making risk临床决策风险

Patients or clinicians may misunderstand the magnitude, timeframe, or clinical meaning of a risk estimate.患者或医生可能误解风险估计的大小、时间范围或临床含义。

Use standardized risk labels; report absolute risk alongside relative risk; specify timeframe, denominator, baseline population, and risk group definition.使用标准化的风险标签；在相对风险旁同时报告绝对风险；写明时间范围、分母、基线人群和风险组的定义。

Full description from the paper论文中的完整描述

The first dimension concerns the semantic content of the risk estimate itself, before considering how that estimate is displayed. Risk estimate ambiguity arises when a probability is reported without information needed to interpret it, such as whether the estimate is absolute or relative, the relevant denominator, or the time horizon. Risk stratification problems arise when categories are overly broad, when thresholds separating low-, medium-, and high-risk groups are not justified, or when category definitions are applied inconsistently. Underlying both is the clinical meaning of risk: an estimate can be technically correct yet still leave clinicians or patients uncertain about what event is being predicted, over what period, and what the estimate implies for a decision.第一个维度关注风险估计本身的语义内容，先不考虑它如何显示。当报告一个概率却缺少理解它所需的信息时，例如它是绝对风险还是相对风险、对应的分母是什么、时间范围多长，就会出现风险估计含糊。当风险类别划分过宽、区分低中高风险组的阈值缺乏依据，或类别定义使用不一致时，就会出现风险分层问题。两者背后都是风险的临床含义：一个估计在技术上可能正确，却仍让医生或患者不清楚预测的是什么事件、在多长时间内，以及这个估计对决策意味着什么。

Uncertainty communication不确定性沟通

What is known about its uncertainty?对它的不确定性了解多少？

11of 47 coded sources as primary dimension篇以此为主维度（共 47 篇编码文献）

Focus关注点

The epistemic status of an estimate: how much uncertainty surrounds it and how that uncertainty should be interpreted.估计的认知状态：它周围有多少不确定性，以及应当如何理解这种不确定性。

Issue types and subtypes问题类型与子类型

  • Uncertainty Omission遗漏不确定性

    • Missing confidence intervals缺少置信区间
    • Missing prediction intervals缺少预测区间
    • No discussion of model uncertainty or uncertainty sources没有讨论模型的不确定性或其来源

  • Uncertainty Interpretation不确定性的解读

    • Unclear distinction between confidence, variability, prediction error, and model limitations没有分清置信度、变异性、预测误差和模型局限

  • Overstated Certainty夸大确定性

    • Risk estimates presented as more precise or definitive than the underlying evidence supports风险估计被呈现得比证据所能支持的更精确或更确定

Clinical decision-making risk临床决策风险

Decision-makers may overtrust or undertrust predictions when confidence, variability, or model limitations are not clearly communicated.当置信度、变异性或模型局限没有被清楚传达时，决策者可能过度信任或过低信任预测。

Display confidence intervals, prediction intervals, uncertainty ranges, or qualitative uncertainty cues; explain major sources of uncertainty in plain language.显示置信区间、预测区间、不确定性范围或定性的不确定性提示；用通俗的语言解释不确定性的主要来源。

These mappings are intended as design hypotheses rather than validated remedies.这些对应关系是设计假设，而不是经过验证的解决方案。

Full description from the paper论文中的完整描述

The second dimension concerns the epistemic status of an estimate: how much uncertainty surrounds it and how that uncertainty should be interpreted. Uncertainty omission occurs when confidence or prediction intervals are absent or when relevant sources of model uncertainty are not communicated. Uncertainty interpretation problems arise when distinctions among sampling uncertainty, outcome variability, prediction error, and model limitations are blurred. At the opposite extreme, overstated certainty presents an estimate as more precise or definitive than the available evidence supports. The studies grouped here collectively emphasize that displaying a number without communicating its limits can invite users to infer a degree of certainty that the underlying evidence does not warrant.第二个维度关注估计的认知状态：它周围有多少不确定性，以及应当如何理解这种不确定性。当缺少置信区间或预测区间，或者没有传达模型不确定性的相关来源时，就是遗漏不确定性。当抽样不确定性、结局变异、预测误差和模型局限之间的区别被模糊时，就会出现不确定性解读问题。另一个极端是夸大确定性：把一个估计呈现得比现有证据所支持的更精确或更确定。归入这一维度的研究共同强调：只显示一个数字而不交代它的局限，可能让使用者推断出证据并不支持的确定程度。

Full description from the paper论文中的完整描述

The third dimension concerns how information is encoded and presented, distinct from the substantive content being communicated. Numeric presentation issues include percentages that are difficult to interpret, inconsistent use of fractions, natural frequencies, and absolute risks, and unnecessary numerical precision. Visual presentation issues include misleading color scales, unclear icon arrays, and poorly labeled charts or risk ladders. Textual explanation issues include vague wording and the absence of a plain-language account of what a display means. The breadth of work in this dimension reflects an established evidence base showing that presentation format can alter comprehension and, intentionally or unintentionally, influence decisions.第三个维度关注信息如何被编码和呈现，与所传达的实质内容相区分。数字呈现方面的问题包括难以理解的百分比，分数、自然频数和绝对风险的使用不一致，以及不必要的数字精度。视觉呈现方面的问题包括有误导性的色阶、不清楚的图标阵列，以及标注不清的图表或风险阶梯。文字说明方面的问题包括措辞含糊、缺少对显示含义的通俗解释。这一维度的研究数量众多，反映出一个已经成熟的证据基础：呈现格式会改变理解，并会有意或无意地影响决策。

Full description from the paper论文中的完整描述

The fourth dimension shifts from the communication artifact to the person interpreting it. Numeracy barriers include difficulty interpreting probabilities and confusion between population-level and individual-level risk. Cognitive burden arises when information volume, display complexity, or time pressure exceeds what a user can process during a clinical encounter. Trust and perceived credibility capture both directions of miscalibrated reliance: overtrusting an automated output because it appears authoritative and underusing a potentially helpful output when its source, rationale, or limitations are unclear. This dimension therefore concerns not simply whether information is available, but how users are likely to understand and respond to it.第四个维度把视角从沟通载体转向解读它的人。数字素养障碍包括难以理解概率，以及混淆人群层面与个体层面的风险。当信息量、显示复杂度或时间压力超出使用者在一次诊疗中能处理的范围时，就会产生认知负担。信任与可信度感知涵盖依赖失准的两个方向：因为输出看起来权威而过度信任，或因为来源、依据或局限不清楚而没有用上一个本可能有帮助的输出。因此，这一维度关心的不只是信息是否存在，更是使用者可能如何理解和回应它。

Full description from the paper论文中的完整描述

The fifth dimension concerns the transition from information to action. Actionability problems arise when an output provides no clear connection to a next step or to the treatment options available in the clinical context. Workflow integration problems include poorly timed delivery, alert fatigue, and failure to fit the sequence and time constraints of the encounter. Recommendation communication problems arise when an automated recommendation is not clearly linked to the clinical choices, treatment options, or decision thresholds it is intended to inform. The defining question for this dimension is whether the communicated output can be incorporated into an actual clinical decision without creating additional ambiguity or workflow burden.第五个维度关注从信息到行动的过渡。当输出与下一步、或与临床情境中可用的治疗选择之间没有清晰联系时，就会出现可操作性问题。工作流程整合问题包括推送时机不当、警报疲劳，以及与诊疗的先后顺序和时间限制不匹配。当自动生成的建议没有与它要支持的临床选择、治疗方案或决策阈值清楚地联系起来时，就会出现建议传达问题。这一维度的核心问题是：所传达的输出能否融入真实的临床决策，而不带来额外的含糊或工作负担。

Full description from the paper论文中的完整描述

The sixth dimension concerns communication about the model itself (its behavior, performance, and appropriate conditions of use). Explanation misinterpretation occurs when feature importance is read as causation or when a simplified explanation obscures uncertainty and model limitations. Model performance communication problems arise when calibration is not explained, when performance metrics are not translated into clinically meaningful consequences, or when false positive and false negative behavior is left unclear. Conditions for appropriate use capture the absence of guidance about when an output should be relied on, interpreted cautiously, or overridden by clinical judgment. This dimension is deliberately broader than explainability: knowing why a model produced an output is not equivalent to knowing whether that output is reliable in the present setting.第六个维度关注关于模型本身的沟通（它的行为、性能和适用条件）。当特征重要性被理解为因果，或简化的解释掩盖了不确定性和模型局限时，就会出现解释被误读。当没有解释校准、性能指标没有转化为有临床意义的后果，或假阳性和假阴性的情况没有说清时，就会出现模型性能传达问题。适用条件指的是缺少指引，说明何时可以依赖输出、何时需谨慎解读、何时应由临床判断推翻。这一维度有意比可解释性更宽：知道模型为什么给出某个输出，并不等于知道这个输出在当前情境下是否可靠。

The seventh dimension concerns whether model reliability is communicated for the populations the tool is intended to serve. Subgroup performance differences arise when a model performs differently across patient groups but that variation is not surfaced to users. Data representativeness issues arise when missing data, biased samples, or underrepresentation of particular populations limit applicability without being made visible. Equity-sensitive interpretation concerns whether users are alerted when a prediction warrants additional caution for an underrepresented or clinically distinct group. Only one coded study primarily informed this dimension, so the category should be treated as an important analytic signpost rather than a mature evidence base within the present corpus.第七个维度关注是否针对工具要服务的人群说明了模型的可靠性。当模型在不同患者群体中表现不同、而这种差异没有呈现给使用者时，就会出现亚组表现差异。当数据缺失、样本偏倚或特定人群代表性不足限制了适用性、却没有被标示出来时，就会出现数据代表性问题。关注公平的解读指的是：当某个预测对代表性不足或临床特征不同的人群需要额外谨慎时，使用者是否会得到提醒。只有一项编码研究以这一维度为主，因此在本研究的文献中，这一类别应被视为一个重要的分析路标，而非成熟的证据基础。

The eighth dimension concerns whether communication supports a decision that reflects the patient's values and preferences. Patient preference integration problems arise when goals and values are absent from how information is presented or interpreted. Trade-off communication problems arise when benefits, harms, uncertainty, and treatment trade-offs are not made sufficiently clear for a preference-sensitive choice. Clinician-patient discussion support concerns whether tools and decision aids provide prompts or structure that help clinicians and patients deliberate together. The focus is therefore not merely on understanding an output, but on whether communication enables that output to contribute appropriately to a shared decision.第八个维度关注沟通是否支持一个反映患者价值观和偏好的决策。当信息的呈现或解读中缺少患者的目标和价值观时，就会出现纳入患者偏好的问题。当获益、危害、不确定性和治疗取舍不够清楚、不足以支持偏好敏感的选择时，就会出现利弊权衡传达问题。医患讨论支持指的是工具和决策辅助是否提供提示或结构，帮助医生和患者一起商议。因此，这一维度的重点不只是看懂输出，而是沟通能否让这个输出恰当地参与到共同决策中。

Uncertainty is a design problem不确定性是一个设计问题

Uncertainty communication is a design problem rather than a disclosure problem alone. An interval or quality of evidence label is useful only to the extent that users can interpret its meaning and incorporate it into a decision.不确定性沟通是一个设计问题，而不只是要不要披露的问题。区间或证据质量标签，只有在使用者能理解其含义并把它纳入决策时才有用。

Explanation and reliability are distinct targets解释与可靠性是两个不同的沟通目标

Model explanation and model reliability are related but distinct communication targets. Feature attribution displays and summary accuracy metrics may improve transparency while providing little information about calibration, transportability, or subgroup performance.模型解释与模型可靠性相关，但是两个不同的沟通目标。特征归因显示和汇总的准确性指标可以提高透明度，却几乎不能说明校准、可迁移性或亚组表现。
